Overview

GelaGO casts locomotion prediction as a graph learning problem. Head pose, gaze direction, eye openness, and avatar motion form the nodes of a per-frame graph, and a Mamba-based sequence encoder models how that graph evolves over time to forecast the avatar's future displacement.

The method is trained and evaluated on recordings from fifteen participants across three locomotion techniques and three virtual scenes, each captured as a synchronized stream of head-mounted display signals sampled at 90 Hz.

Dataset at a glance

15Participants
3 × 3Techniques × Scenes
130Synchronized recordings
21Features per frame

Arm-Swing, Touchpad, and Teleport, each recorded in the Maze, Park, and Village scenes. Every recording is a single CSV file with one row per sampled frame.
